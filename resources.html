RESEARCH AND CREDIBILITY

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy Resources

Find clarity on the many uses of HBOT, from conditions approved by the FDA to areas of wellness currently explored in clinical research.

While HBOT is not the primary treatment for many injuries and illnesses, it is known to help with a wide variety of health and wellness issues.

FDA Approved Conditions

  • Acute anemia
  • Air & gas embolism
  • Carbon monoxide poisoning
  • Central retinal artery occlusion
  • Chronic refractory osteomyelitis
  • Crush injury & other acute traumatic ischemias
  • Decompression sickness
  • Diabetic and non-healing wounds
  • Failed skin grafts and flaps
  • Gas gangrene
  • Necrotizing tissue infections
  • Radiation damage
  • Sudden sensorineural hearing loss
  • Thermal burns

Off-Label / Wellness Support

  • Arthritis
  • Anti-aging / Longevity
  • Addiction recovery
  • Alzheimers
  • Autism
  • Autoimmunity
  • Bacterial and viral infections
  • Cancer
  • Cellulitis
  • Concussions
  • Crohn's disease
  • Chronic fatigue syndrome
  • Chronic pain & migraine
  • Dementia
  • Dental infections and healing
  • ED (Erectile Dysfunction)
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Inflammatory bowel disease
  • Long COVID
  • Mold exposure
  • Lyme disease
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Plastic surgery healing
  • POTS
  • PTSD/TBI
  • Sports injuries
  • Stem cells support
  • Stroke recovery
  • Surgery preparation & recovery
  • Ulcerative colitis
