How to Book a Consult or Session

At ART Hyperbarics, your safety and results come first. Before your first hyperbaric session, all new patients are required to complete a 30-minute medical consult with one of our doctors.

New Patient Consult Details

This ensures we create a safe, effective, and fully customized treatment plan designed specifically for your health goals.

Duration

30 minutes

Cost

$50

To Determine:
  • Proper Pressure Levels
  • Session Frequency
  • Personalized Protocol
1

Call to Schedule

To book your consult, please contact ART Hyperbarics:

(770) 321-0696

Our team will confirm your appointment and guide you through the next steps.

2

Patient Portal

After scheduling your consult, you will receive a secure link to create your ART Hyperbarics patient account.

Inside your personal portal, you can:

  • Complete medical history and treatment forms
  • Explain your reason for treatment
  • Review and sign liability releases
  • Securely place a credit card on file
  • View upcoming sessions, payment information, and visit history

This allows our doctors to review everything in advance, giving you more time during the consult to focus on your goals, questions, and individualized protocol.
