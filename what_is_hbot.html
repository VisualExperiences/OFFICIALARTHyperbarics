What Is Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT)?

The Therapy Explained

Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) is a medical treatment that delivers pure oxygen in a pressurized hard-shell chamber. By increasing atmospheric pressure, your lungs are able to absorb significantly more oxygen than is possible under normal conditions. This oxygen is then dissolved directly into the bloodstream and delivered throughout the body—including areas with reduced circulation.

At ART Hyperbarics, we utilize medical-grade hard chambers designed to safely and efficiently deliver oxygen at therapeutic pressures, allowing oxygen to penetrate deeper into tissues, cells, and even areas with compromised blood flow.

How Does Hyperbaric Therapy Work?

Key Physiological Benefits:

When the body is exposed to increased pressure while breathing pure oxygen, several key benefits occur:

  • Oxygen delivery to tissues increases dramatically
  • Cellular energy (ATP) production improves
  • Inflammation is reduced
  • The body’s natural healing and repair processes are enhanced
  • Stem cell activity and angiogenesis (new blood vessel formation) are supported

This combination helps the body heal more efficiently and recover faster from injury, inflammation, neurological stress, and chronic conditions.
