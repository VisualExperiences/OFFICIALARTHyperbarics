What Is Cold Laser Therapy?

What is the Erchonia Cold Laser?

The Erchonia Cold Laser is a Low-Level Laser Therapy (LLLT) device. Unlike surgical or “hot” lasers that cut or burn tissue, this laser uses very low levels of light—but are a high energy—that don’t produce heat.

How Does It Work? (Cellular Power Up)

1

Light Delivery

The laser shines a specific wavelength of light onto the skin, penetrating deep into the cells.

2

Mitochondria Stimulation

This light stimulates the mitochondria (the “power plants” of cells).

3

ATP Production

The mitochondria respond by making more ATP (adenosine triphosphate), which is the cell’s main source of energy.

With more energy, cells can:

  • Repair themselves faster
  • Reduce inflammation
  • Improve circulation
  • Promote tissue healing
