SAFETY, COMFORT, AND RELIABILITY

Our Chambers: BaraMed Select

At ART Hyperbarics, we proudly feature BaraMed Select Chambers, recognized for their advanced engineering, safety standards, and superior patient comfort.

Why We Trust BaraMed Select

These chambers are engineered to provide a safe, comfortable, and effective hyperbaric environment, ensuring the highest standard of safety and care for our patients.

Advanced Pressure Capabilities

Safely reach up to 3 atmospheres (30 psi), providing the full range of therapeutic pressures needed for effective treatments.

Enhanced Communication

Equipped with built-in telephones, allowing for seamless, two-way communication between patients and staff at all times.

Clear Visibility

The clear acrylic outer shell offers full visual contact with staff, enhancing both safety and patient reassurance.

Patient Comfort & Space

With ample space and comfortable medical-grade mattress, these chambers prioritize a pleasant and relaxing treatment experience.

Our Commitment to Safety

At ART Hyperbarics, we trust BaraMed Select Chambers to deliver outstanding therapeutic benefits and ensure the highest standard of safety and care.

The staff at ART Hyperbarics has routine checklists completed on each chamber, daily. Additionally, we have BARA-MED Select Technicians visit annually to extensively maintain each and every chamber, as well as recertify them through registered officials within Georgia's State Department.
